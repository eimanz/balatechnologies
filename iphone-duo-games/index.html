Games for iPhone Duo

Games that use the hinge, not just the two screens. Both play on every other iPhone and on iPad too.

Uphill: Ball Roll Arcade Game
Stand the phone up like a small laptop: the lower half is the lane, the raised half is the board, and the hinge's angle sets the slope. A steeper board pays up to ×2.4. Five machines, tickets and a daily challenge; one price, no ads, no in-app purchases.
InkBlot: Folded Ink Art
Drop ink on the page, close the phone to press the pages together, and open it to peel the print, the way an ink blot is really made.

Why the hinge

iPhone Duo's hinge reports its angle as you fold it. Uphill turns that angle into the slope of an arcade board, so you play it the way you'd lean over a real machine; InkBlot turns the fold itself into the fold of the paper.
