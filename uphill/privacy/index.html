Uphill Duo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026. Applies from version 1.0.1; version 1.0 sent nothing at all.

Uphill Duo: Roll Ball Game (“Uphill”) is a game published by Bala Technologies LLC. It has no accounts and no ads, and it doesn't share or sell information about you. The only thing it sends anywhere is anonymous statistics about how it is played, which you can turn off.

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in and no account.
  • No ads and no tracking. Uphill shows no ads and doesn't follow you across other apps or websites.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. To learn how Uphill is played and make it better, the app sends anonymous statistics to PostHog, an analytics service: that the app was opened and for how long, how each ball was rolled (a flick, a hold or a tap, how hard, and the slope it was rolled at), where it ended up and what it scored, each game's score, which screens were visited and which settings were changed. A few technical details go with them: the device model, system version, screen size and language, whether it's on Wi-Fi or cellular, the app's name and version, whether it came from the App Store or TestFlight, a random number for each visit, and the version of PostHog's software in the app. Each installation is known only by a random number made up on the device. It isn't tied to your name, your Apple Account or anything else about you. Your location isn't collected: the app asks PostHog not to look up where the connection comes from, internet addresses are discarded, and the device's time zone is left out. You can turn the statistics off at any time in Settings, under Share anonymous usage statistics. That stops the recording at once: what was already recorded may still be sent, and after that the app doesn't contact PostHog at all.
  • What stays on your device. Your best score and your settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, and you can reset your best score in Settings.
  • The hinge. On iPhone Duo, Uphill reads the angle of the hinge to set the slope of the ramp while you play. It also remembers what the angle reads when the phone is fully open, so the slope is right the next time. The slope each ball was rolled at, whether the hinge or the slider set it, and how the phone was folded (like a laptop, flat, or like a book) are part of the anonymous statistics. Nothing else about the hinge leaves the device.
  • Children. Uphill isn't directed at children, and the anonymous statistics are the same for everyone.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page, with a new date.
