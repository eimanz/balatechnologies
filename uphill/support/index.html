How to play

  • Flick up from the lane at the bottom of the screen. The ball crosses the crease and climbs the ramp. You can also touch and hold Roll, then let go: the longer you hold, the harder the roll.
  • A ball that slows down over a hole drops in. Higher holes score more, and the 100s sit in the top corners.
  • You get nine balls a game. Your best score is kept on your phone.

The slope

  • iPhone Duo: set the phone on a table with the bottom half flat, like a laptop. The flat half is the lane and the raised half is the ramp. Stand the lid up for a steeper ramp: harder rolls and a bigger score multiplier. The slope locks while a ball is rolling.
  • iPad and other iPhones, or if you'd rather not use the hinge: drag the slope slider. On iPhone Duo you can switch to the slider in Settings.

Common questions

  • There's no sound. On iPhone, sound follows the Ring/Silent switch; on iPad, the volume and mute settings. Check Settings → Sound effects is on.
  • How do I reset my best score? Settings → Reset best score.
  • The slope doesn't follow the hinge. Open Settings and check Slope control is set to Hinge. Fold slowly; the slope eases to the new angle.
  • Which devices are supported? Any iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later. On iPad it plays full screen in any orientation, and in Split View, Slide Over and Stage Manager. The hinge controls need iPhone Duo.
  • Can I play with a keyboard? Yes: on an iPad with a keyboard, Space rolls the ball and Return starts a new game.
