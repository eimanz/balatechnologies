How to play

  • Flick up from the lane at the bottom of the screen. You can also touch and hold Roll, then let go: the longer you hold, the harder the roll.
  • The game opens on the arcade floor. Swipe across it to change machine, then tap Play. The back arrow on a machine's screen takes you back to the floor, and a game left part-way waits for you there.
  • You get nine balls a game, and every machine keeps its own best score, on your device and, if you're signed in to Game Center, on its leaderboard.

The five machines

  • Rings: the ball jumps off the end of the lane and comes down among the rings. Higher rings score more: 50 at the top, and the 100s in the corners. Every ball rolls down into something.
  • Pockets: the ball crosses the crease and climbs the ramp, and drops into a hole if it slows down over one. The 100s sit in the top corners.
  • Hot Shot: sixty seconds on the clock. Roll as many balls as you can before time runs out.
  • Daily: the same board for everyone each day, at a slope set for the day, with lit rings that pay triple. Your first game of the day is the one that counts (later games are practice); it goes on a leaderboard that starts afresh every day, and playing on more days in a row builds a streak.
  • Trick Alley: the rings with a trick ball. Super-Bounce Rubber comes free; Heavy Brass and Spin are at the prize counter.

Tickets and prizes

  • Every game pays tickets: one for every 10 points (every 50 on Hot Shot), and more for 100s, the Daily and the Trick-Shot Tour.
  • Trade them at the prize counter for new balls. Balls change only how the ball looks; trick balls, for Trick Alley, change how it rolls. A few balls can't be bought, only won with a feat.
  • The Trick-Shot Tour is thirty shots to make in order, from your first 30 to three 100s in one game. Each pays more tickets than the last.
  • Tickets are never sold. Uphill is one price, with no in-app purchases.

The slope

  • iPhone Duo: set the phone on a table with the bottom half flat, like a laptop. The flat half is the lane and the raised half is the board. Stand the lid up for a steeper board: a bigger score multiplier, but a harder target. The slope locks while a ball is rolling.
  • iPad and other iPhones, or if you'd rather not use the hinge: drag the slope slider. On iPhone Duo you can switch to the slider in Settings.

Common questions

  • There's no sound. On iPhone, sound follows the Ring/Silent switch; on iPad, the volume and mute settings. Check Settings → Sound effects is on.
  • How do I reset my best score? Play the machine, then Settings → Reset best score. It resets that machine's score on your device; Game Center keeps its own record.
  • My scores aren't on Game Center. Check that you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Your best scores and achievements are sent again the next time Uphill opens while you're signed in and online.
  • I lost my tickets. Tickets and prizes are kept on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. They come back with a restore from a device backup, but they don't move between devices on their own.
  • What is the free try? The Uphill App Clip lets you roll three balls on the Rings machine before you buy, with nothing to install. Your score there stays in the App Clip.
  • The slope doesn't follow the hinge. Open Settings and check Slope control is set to Hinge. Fold slowly; the slope eases to the new angle.
  • Which devices are supported? Any iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later. On iPad it plays full screen in any orientation, and in Split View, Slide Over and Stage Manager. The hinge controls need iPhone Duo.
  • Can I play with a keyboard? Yes: on an iPad with a keyboard, Space rolls the ball and Return starts a new game.
