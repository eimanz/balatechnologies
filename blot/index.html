Ink blot prints, made for iPhone Duo. Drop ink on the left page, then close your Duo: the pages press together, and the longer it stays shut, the further the ink spreads. Open it to see the mirrored print.

Each level pencils a shape on the paper, from a bow tie to a beetle. Choose where the drops go, how big they are and how long to press, and fill the shape for three stars. Or take a free sheet and all four inks: India black, carmine, Prussian blue and sepia.

The ink is simulated drop by drop on cotton paper: it soaks in, spreads along the fibres, bleeds into its neighbours, tears into veins as the pages part and dries where it lies. No two prints are the same. On any other iPhone, fold the page over on screen.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing leaves your phone.
