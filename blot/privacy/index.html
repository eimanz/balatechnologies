InkBlot Duo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

InkBlot Duo (“Blot”) is a game published by Bala Technologies LLC. It does not collect, share or sell any information about you, and it doesn't send anything to a server.

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in and no account.
  • No network access. Blot makes no network connections.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. Blot contains no third-party code.
  • What stays on your device. Your best scores and your settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The hinge. On iPhone Duo, Blot reads whether the phone is open or shut, to press the pages together. Nothing about it leaves the device.
  • Sharing. When you share a print, Blot hands the picture to the share sheet and you choose where it goes. Blot itself sends nothing.
  • Children. Blot collects no information from anyone, including children.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page, with a new date.
