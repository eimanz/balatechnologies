How to play

  • Touch the left page to let a drop of ink fall. Hold your finger down longer for a bigger drop. Tap an ink on the tray to change colour.
  • iPhone Duo: close the phone to press the pages together, and open it to see the print.
  • Any iPhone: tap Fold, or drag the left page's outer edge across the fold. Tap Open to lift it.
  • The longer the sheet stays shut, the further the ink spreads. Fill the pencilled shape and keep inside its lines: the stamp shows how close you came, and three stars is a match.

Common questions

  • The ink didn't print. Only wet ink prints. A drop stays wet for about twenty seconds, so press soon after dropping it.
  • There's no sound. Sound follows the Ring/Silent switch, and can be turned off in Settings.
  • Can I keep a print? Tap the share button after a print to save or send the picture.
  • Which iPhones are supported? Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Closing the phone to press needs iPhone Duo.
