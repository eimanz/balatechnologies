Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea for a game or a category? Email balatechnologiesllc@gmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and the language you play in.

Getting started

  • Pick a game on the home screen. Each game's setup screen has a How to play button with the rules, step by step.
  • Add players or teams, choose categories, and pass the phone around.
  • Change the language in Settings → Language: Persian, English, Arabic or Turkish.

YehDor Studio and Pro

  • YehDor Studio lets you make your own categories and words. They appear right in the games that use them.
  • Free, you can make one category per game with up to 15 words. YehDor Studio Pro is a one-time purchase: no limits, editing of YehDor's own categories, and no ads.
  • Bought Pro on another device, or reinstalled? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

  • How do I turn on 18+ content? Enter your date of birth in Settings; if you are 18 or over, the 18+ Adult Content switch becomes available. It is off by default and never available in Kids Mode.
  • How do I lock Kids Mode? Switching Kids Mode off asks for Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode, so an adult has to approve it.
  • There's no sound. Check the Ring/Silent switch and the volume, then check that sound is on in YehDor's Settings.
  • Where are my custom categories stored? Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them, so keep that in mind before reinstalling.
  • Which devices are supported? iPhone with iOS 15.5 or later.
