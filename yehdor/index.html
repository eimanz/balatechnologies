Party games for one phone in the middle of the table. YehDor (یه‌دور, “one round”) gathers thirteen group games in one app: word guessing against the clock, Pantomime, Spy, Mafia, Taboo, Truth or Dare, Bottle Spin, Never Have I Ever, Who Am I?, Story Chain, Bomb, Two Truths and a Lie and Cheshmak. Pass the phone around; nobody needs an account.

Play in Persian, English, Arabic or Turkish. Make your own categories and words in YehDor Studio and they show up right in the games. Family and Kids modes keep the content right for the room.

Everything you type stays on your phone. YehDor Studio Pro, a one-time purchase, removes ads and lifts the limits on your own categories.
