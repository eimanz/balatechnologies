YehDor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

YehDor (یه‌دور) is a party-games app for a group sharing one phone, published by Bala Technologies LLC. It has no accounts or sign-in, and we run no server: we receive nothing you type into it. Free games can show ads, which YehDor Studio Pro removes. This page describes what stays on your phone and what Google's components and Apple receive.

What stays on your phone

All of this is kept in the app's own storage on your device and never sent anywhere:

  • Player and team names, and saved rosters.
  • The categories and words you make in YehDor Studio.
  • Game history and the local leaderboard.
  • Your settings: language, theme, sound and vibration, audience mode, and the order of games on the home screen.
  • The date of birth you enter for the 18+ content gate.
  • Who Am I? replays (see Camera and microphone, below).

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We keep no backup and cannot restore it.

What leaves your phone

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). Free games can show a Google AdMob ad after a game ends, and you can choose to watch an ad to open the 18+ categories. YehDor Studio Pro removes ads, and Kids Mode never requests or shows them. Ad requests send Google device and network information (including the internet address), how you interact with ads, and diagnostics. YehDor asks Google for non-personalized ads only, and never asks to track you or to read your device's advertising identifier. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and your privacy choices stay available in Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • On-device translation (Google ML Kit). If you turn on an AI feature in a game's setup, YehDor writes the content with Apple's on-device model and translates it with Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. The first time for each language, ML Kit downloads a language pack (about 30 MB) from Google; after that, translation works offline. ML Kit reports a device identifier to Google for analytics and to make the component work; per Google's privacy manifest it isn't linked to your identity or used to track you. The text being translated, your player names and your categories are processed on the phone and not sent. If you never turn on an AI feature, ML Kit is never used. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Purchases. YehDor Studio Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's In-App Purchase. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether you own it, so it can unlock it and restore it on your other devices.
  • Links. The Mafia rules offer reference links (such as Wikipedia). Tapping one opens Safari and leaves the app; those sites have their own policies.

Device permissions

  • Camera and microphone. Only if you turn on replays in Who Am I?. The front camera films the room during the round, with sound if you allow the microphone, so you can watch it back afterwards. Replays stay on your phone unless you choose to share or save one.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Only so a child can't switch Kids Mode off without an adult. iOS performs the check and tells YehDor only whether it passed; YehDor never receives biometric data.
  • Photos (add only). Only when you choose to save a results card or a replay. YehDor can add that item and cannot see your photo library.

Children and mature content

YehDor isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone. Family and Kids modes limit which games and categories appear, and Kids Mode shows no ads. Some content is for adults and sits behind an 18+ gate: it appears only if the date of birth you enter shows you are 18 or over and you switch adult content on. That date of birth stays on your phone.

Your choices

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. You control all of it: clear items in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything. For information Google holds, use the links above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date, and a material change will be noted in the App Store release notes.
